<details><summary>How these are detected</summary>

- **Allay Express** — Pure criteria, no function. 'courier' is allay_drop_item_on_block with exactly the location shape vanilla's Birthday Song and Enhanced Discoveries' Uncontrolled Reproduction use - a location_check on minecraft:note_block plus a match_tool on the carried item, here Paper. 'posted' is the Paper leaving your hand into another player's, with the distance-min-1 thrower guard. DELIBERATE CHANGE: the two halves are not linked to each other. The original wanted the Allay's delivery itself picked up by a second player, which needs a UUID match in a function; worse, I am not confident an Allay-dropped ItemEntity carries a player thrower at all, and if it does not, thrown_item_picked_up_by_player would never fire on the receiver and the advancement would be dead. So the receiving half is a plain hand-over of Paper instead, which is guaranteed to work. What the pack sees is 'you ran an Allay post office and you handed post to somebody' - unlinked, like Pay It Forward's two halves.
- **Bedside Manner** — Pure criteria, no function. 'cured' is the bare cured_zombie_villager trigger BACAP's Global Vaccination uses (there with a VillagerData nbt filter; unfiltered here, any profession will do). 'supplied' is a plain Golden Apple - not an Enchanted one, which is what the three existing hand-me-an-apple entries in this pack use - taken off another player's drop with the distance-min-1 guard, and it is the half that cannot be done alone. DELIBERATE CHANGE: the original had the OTHER player trade with the cured villager inside a minute, which needs the villager UUID stored and a timer. Here the doctor supplies neither the apple nor the customer: he takes the apple from a partner and trades with the patient himself. Nothing ties the trade to the villager that was cured, and there is no time window.
- **Care Package** — Four minecraft:thrown_item_picked_up_by_entity criteria, one per Iron piece, each requiring the picker to be a player at least one block from the thrower - the self-exclusion idiom from mining/diamonds_to_you, because this trigger has none of its own and a dropped item you walk back onto fires it at distance 0. requirements is an AND of the four, so all four pieces must reach somebody else's hands. The ten-second window in the original idea is dropped: a timer would have cost a detection function and the four-piece AND already cannot be done alone.
- **Charity Case** — Pure criteria, no detector. Four minecraft:thrown_item_picked_up_by_player criteria, one per armour slot, each requiring the thrower to be a player at distance min 1 so nothing you drop for yourself counts. All four are ANDed by default. Every armour ID was checked line by line against VALID_IDS.txt, including this version's copper set and turtle_helmet. Documented weakening: the spare asked for four DIFFERENT donors and for the set to end up worn. Neither is expressible in criteria — an advancement cannot see donor identity, and I had no verified example of an equipment-slot predicate on a location trigger to lean on — so the requirement is four pieces from other players, players is set to 2 rather than 5, and the tier is dropped from challenge to goal to match the reduced difficulty.
- **Closing Time** — Pure criteria, no function. 'bought' is villager_trade with BOTH the villager and item conditions, exactly as BACAP's Florist and Rebuilding the Oceans pin a Wandering Trader's specific stock; Packed Ice and Blue Ice are both listed so the criterion survives whichever of the two the trader happens to be offering. 'funded' and 'delivered' are the two directions of the hand-over idiom with the distance-min-1 guard, and either one alone is impossible solo. DELIBERATE CHANGE: 'the same trader, before he despawns' needs the trader's UUID matched across two players in a function. The despawn timer is not read at all - what makes this feel like closing time is that the ice has to be bought and passed on, not how long the trader lives. UNVERIFIED: I have not read the trade tables in this exact version; if a Wandering Trader in 1.21.9 offers neither ice, 'bought' is unattainable and the entry should be repointed at coral blocks, which BACAP already relies on in Rebuilding the Oceans.
- **Diamond Hands** — One vanilla criterion, the exact shape of BACAP's mining/diamonds_to_you but with the receiver pinned to entity_type minecraft:player and the stack pinned to count 64, so a mob picking it up and a single loose diamond both miss. The distance absolute min 1 guard is what excludes the thrower picking his own drop back up, since he is always at distance 0 from himself. Raised from the original one-diamond idea because 'Diamonds to you!' already covers throwing a diamond at another player or mob; a whole stack and a player-only receiver is what makes it a new advancement.
- **Distraction Tactics** — No detector. `rang` copies Enhanced Discoveries adventure/din_don exactly - default_block_use with a location_check on minecraft:bell - which is how a bare-handed bell ring is caught. The pairing runs in the FAST bucket, once per tick rather than every ten, because the rescued player's health is read live and half a second is long enough for them to heal or die. At the ringer's position it requires at least one #minecraft:raiders entity within 30 blocks (the same entity tag BACAP's own sound_the_alarm check uses, and the selector is radius-bounded), then grants to every non-spectator 1..30 blocks away sitting at 5 or less on the declared bacaptmh_m1hp health objective, then to the ringer, then clears the hook. distance=1..30 excludes the ringer, so it takes two players. Honest limit: the raiders' Target field is not read - what is enforced is a bell rung with raiders in range while a teammate is genuinely under three hearts, not a proven change of aggro.
- **Double Booking** — Pure criteria, no function. 'traded' is villager_trade with the villager entity_type pinned, the shape BACAP's Travelling Merchant uses (with wandering_trader) - an ordinary trade with an ordinary Villager. 'handed_over' is the diamonds_to_you drop-and-be-collected idiom with the distance-min-1 guard, which is the half that cannot be done alone. DELIBERATE CHANGE: the original wanted two players trading with the SAME villager inside ten seconds, which needs the villager's UUID stored per player and compared in a fast-bucket function; the batch's one detector went to Going, Going, Gone. What is left is the same fiction from the villager's side - his stock ends up in two players' hands - with nothing tying the traded item to the item handed over, and no time window between them.
- **Dress Code** — Every 5 s the armour material is read with `execute if items` over the four armour slots and the trim material is read from the equipment NBT of the same four slots, giving each player a material index and a trim index. Only `equipment.head/chest/legs/feet` is read: that is the attested player layout at this pack format - bacaped:animal/youre_part_of_a_hive_mind_now tests player NBT as Inventory slots 0-35 plus an `equipment` compound - so the old Inventory slot 100-103 fallback was dead code and doubled the cost of the scan for nothing. Three players inside 16 blocks who share both indices are granted; the proximity is what makes it a dress code rather than three strangers in three dimensions. Live reads replace advancement criteria, so players who already hold it keep counting towards everyone else's three. It keys on trim material, not pattern, exactly as BACAP's own Coordinated Flair does.
- **Drop Shipping** — No function needed after all: the distance predicate inside entity is measured from the thrower, so distance.y min 100 means the receiver must be a hundred blocks of vertical separation away at the moment he picks the parcel up — the thrower has to still be up the tower when it lands. The y field is an absolute difference (Enhanced Discoveries uses it the same way in adventure/nobody_needs_rockets), so a receiver a hundred blocks ABOVE also counts; that is a strictly harder way to earn it and I left it in rather than adding a function to forbid it. Any item qualifies, since a hundred blocks of vertical separation is not something two players hit by accident.
- **Dying Wish** — Pure criteria, no detector. 'gave' is minecraft:thrown_item_picked_up_by_entity, the trigger bacaptmh:adventure/hot_potato already uses, restricted to a player collector at distance min 1 so items you drop and re-collect never count. 'killed' is minecraft:entity_killed_player in the shape of blazeandcave:adventure/rekt, with the killer pinned to a player at distance min 1 — that distance guard matters here because entity_killed_player has no self-exclusion and a projectile of your own coming back down would otherwise satisfy it. Honest limitations, stated plainly: the 'entire inventory' and 'within fifteen seconds' parts of the idea are both dropped. I had no verified example of an empty-inventory slot predicate to copy, and ordering needs a detector. On a busy PvP server the two criteria can therefore be met incidentally rather than as a deliberate ritual.
- **Escort Mission** — Function-granted. bacaptmh_walkd is the player's walk_one_cm + sprint_one_cm delta for the window; both mirrors are seeded in seed_lines and both deltas are rebuilt for EVERY player, ungated, at the top of bucket_medium, so a player the gate has never touched can never have a lifetime statistic read as one window of travel. detect/escort then runs only for non-spectator, non-creative players who lack the advancement: if the head slot is not a Carved Pumpkin, or a real head count of non-spectators within 5 blocks is under 2, bacaptmh_esc is zeroed and the run returns, so any break in the escort restarts the tally from zero. Otherwise a delta inside 1..2000 cm (positive, and still physically possible on foot in half a second, which also discards the stale mirror a gamemode round trip leaves behind) is added; at 20000 cm the advancement is granted to every non-spectator within 5 blocks, guide included. The guide is not required to be the same player throughout - any escort within 5 blocks keeps the tally alive.
- **Even Split** — Collapsed to one atomic criterion instead of the two the idea sketched: at the instant the other player picks up the drop, the thrown stack must be exactly 32 gold ingots and the thrower's own mainhand must hold exactly 32 more, read through the same player.equipment.mainhand predicate BACAP uses in weaponry/backfire. Both halves are therefore checked at the same tick with no timing window to get wrong. The one way to miss is dropping the 32 onto other loose gold ingots, because item entities merge and the stack on the ground would no longer be exactly 32.
- **Fair Trade** — `gave` fires when an item you threw is picked up by another player and `got` fires when you pick up an item another player threw; both carry `entity.distance.absolute.min = 1`, the guard BACAP uses in mining/diamonds_to_you, so picking your own dropped item back up (distance 0) latches neither. Every 0.5 s the detector stamps a 5-second timer on each flag and revokes it, and grants to any player holding both timers at once - which means an item of theirs went to somebody else and somebody else's item came to them inside the same window. Only the claimant needs live criteria, so a partner who already owns the advancement can still trade with a newcomer. Both timers are cleared on the grant.
- **Fashion Police** — Reuses the same live trim index Dress Code computes. Around each trimmed player the detector tags exactly one wearer per trim material - the same one-slot-per-category trick Split the Party uses for dimensions - and grants only to the tagged five or more once five different materials are present within 32 blocks. Crediting every trimmed player in range would have handed the challenge to seven people wearing five materials between them, which is not what the description says. A single player can only ever hold one index, because the index is only set when all four pieces carry the same material, so five tags means five people. Nothing depends on advancement criteria, so a player who already owns it still supplies their colour to the line-up instead of being invisible to the count.
- **Ferryman** — Function-granted by detect/ferry (mode 1), dispatched every medium tick as every non-spectator, non-creative player who is missing it. The detector takes that player's boat_one_cm delta against a private mirror (bacaptmh_boatm, seeded in seed.mcfunction the first time the pack sees a player, so a lifetime total can never read as one window's travel and the delta is additionally capped at 20000 cm per window). It then walks 'on vehicle ... on passengers' - players are never written into a vehicle's Passengers NBT, so the walk is the only reliable way to see who else is aboard - and looks for a passenger who is not the rower (excluded with a per-call tag, not with a distance test), whose bacaptmh_dtime death stamp is newer than 12000 ticks, and who fails 'if items' on container.*, all four armour slots and weapon.offhand, i.e. is carrying literally nothing. While such a passenger is aboard the delta accumulates into bacaptmh_ferry; the moment the condition breaks the accumulator is reset to 0, so the thousand blocks must be one unbroken ride. At 100000 cm every non-spectator player in that boat is granted, rower and rescued alike. Honest limits: the ride does not have to be in a straight line or end anywhere in particular, and a passenger who dies, empties themselves deliberately and climbs in qualifies just as well as a genuine rescue.
- **Fire Sale** — thrown_item_picked_up_by_player puts the receiver in the player field and the thrower in entity, so the burning man is the achiever and flags.is_on_fire is read off him exactly as BACAP reads it in weaponry/backfire. The idea's second leg — actually putting yourself out with the bucket — was deliberately dropped: buckets place their fluid through the item's own use handler, not through the block-use path that minecraft:item_used_on_block fires from, so there is no attested trigger for it and I would have been inventing one. What ships checks the handover under fire, which is the whole joke anyway.
- **Going, Going, Gone** — THE ONE DETECTOR OF THIS BATCH, function-granted. Both criteria carry the diamonds_to_you distance-absolute-min-1 thrower guard, so neither can be armed by dropping and re-collecting your own Emeralds. 'bid' fires on a SELLER (somebody else picked his Emeralds up); bucket_fast opens a 600-tick window (bacaptmh_bid) on him and revokes the criterion in the same tick so it cannot re-fire every tick. 'won' fires on the BUYER; detect/going_going_gone revokes it first, then counts, with 'execute store result ... if entity' (never 'if entity @a[limit=3]'), how many non-spectators within eight blocks still have a live window, and grants to all of them plus @s at three or more. Three players are genuinely required and sufficient: A gives to B, B gives to C, C gives to A, and on that last pickup all three windows are live at once, so A trips the check with a count of three including himself. Two players can only ever reach a count of two. Honest limitations: the original called for strictly increasing piles and a single block, and neither survived - piles are not sized (the trigger reports the item type, not the stack that moved) and the floor is an eight-block radius rather than one block. What the pack sees is three people passing Emeralds around one spot inside thirty seconds.
- **Group Nap** — Sleeping is picked up two ways: the `slept` criterion for players who still need the advancement, and the vanilla sleep_in_bed statistic against a mirror for those who already hold it and can no longer latch criteria. Either one stamps a 60-second window on the player, and the grant fires when every non-spectator player online holds a live window at the same time. A real-time window replaces the old 'wipe the latch whenever daytime is under 1000' rule, so a frozen or externally driven clock cannot let people sleep sessions apart and still collect. The mirror is seeded at load and refreshed by an ungated bucket line, and it is not shared with any other advancement.
- **Hot Potato** — The check is driven from a middle link - a player who both received an item from someone and had an item of theirs picked up by someone inside the same 10 seconds, which is what being in the middle of a relay produces and which the min-distance-1 guard stops you from faking with your own item. The chain around that link is givers, plus receivers who did not give, plus players who already hold the advancement and whose Items Dropped statistic rose inside the same 10 seconds - the only live channel a holder still has, since a granted advancement stops latching criteria. Simply being present is not enough for anybody. It counts hand-offs, not item identity, so it cannot prove the same potato made the whole trip. All stamps are zeroed on the grant.
- **Inheritance** — 'thirty_levels' copies BACAP statistics/giga_xp_grinder.json verbatim apart from the number: a minecraft:location trigger reading minecraft:type_specific/player level, which the location trigger re-evaluates every 20 ticks. 'handed_down' is the diamonds_to_you thrower guard applied to Bottles o' Enchanting - entity_type player with distance min 1, which self-excludes because your own throw is at distance 0 from you. The original wanted experience orbs tagged with a dead player's UUID, which nothing in criteria can see and which no amount of function would make reliable; the replacement keeps the shape of the idea - somebody else's experience becoming yours - through the one XP carrier the game lets a player physically hand over. The two criteria latch independently, so the levels do not have to come from those specific bottles; the description says 'off', not 'entirely from'.
- **Lost and Found** — Two pure criteria ANDed. 'went_down' is minecraft:entity_killed_player with no conditions, the trigger BACAP uses in adventure/rekt.json - it fires only on a real death caused by an entity, so falling in a hole does not count. 'brought_back' is thrown_item_picked_up_by_player on a Recovery Compass whose thrower is a player at distance at least 1; the thrower of your own drop is measured against your own position and is always at distance 0, so the guard self-excludes exactly as mining/diamonds_to_you does. The original idea wanted a full inventory snapshot reconciled through a third party inside 2400 ticks and was dropped as a function-layer state machine; what survives is the part the game can actually see. The two criteria latch independently and are not time-linked - a death last week and a compass handed over today still complete it, the same looseness BACAP accepts throughout.
- **Market Day** — Pure criteria, no function. The four trade criteria use the VillagerData nbt string verbatim from BACAP's Master Trader, minus its level:5 clause, so any tier of Farmer, Butcher, Cartographer and Mason counts; NBT matching is subset matching, so leaving type and level out is correct rather than sloppy. 'funded' is the Emerald hand-over with the distance-min-1 guard and is the only half that cannot be done alone. DELIBERATE CHANGE: the original wanted four DIFFERENT players trading four different careers inside one village in a minute. Pure criteria can prove that another player was involved but never that four distinct players were - every criterion is evaluated against one player's own history - so players drops from four to two and the village-radius and one-minute clauses are gone with the detector. What survives is the shape of a market day: four stalls worked in one trip on a partner's money.
- **Matching Outfits** — Every 5 s each player's four armour slots are read live with `execute if items`, matching the leather item in each slot together with an exact minecraft:dyed_color value, so the check really is leather armour and not merely something carrying that component - the omission BACAP never makes in animal/fashion_statement. The matched colour index is written to a score and any two players inside 16 blocks sharing it are granted; the proximity is what makes it an outfit rather than two strangers in two dimensions. Because nothing lives in advancement criteria, a player who already holds it still counts as somebody's matching partner. Only the sixteen exact vanilla dye values are recognised, not custom mixed shades.
- **Orchestra** — A horn blast is detected from the vanilla used:goat_horn statistic against a per-cycle mirror rather than from advancement criteria, which is what lets a player who already owns the advancement still play their part; the mirror is seeded at load and refreshed by an ungated bucket line, so it can never read a lifetime total as a fresh blast, and it is not shared with the jump or sleep mirrors. The blower's held horn is inspected with `execute if items` to find which of the eight instruments sounded, that instrument's flag is raised, the player is tagged and the window is opened. The window is fixed, not rolling: only the first blast of a chain sets the five-second timer and later blasts do not extend it, so four instruments really do have to sound inside five seconds of the first one rather than inside a chain of blasts spaced under five seconds apart. It grants when four different instrument flags and four different tagged players are live at once, so one player blowing four horns is not an orchestra. Every flag, the timer and the tags are cleared on the grant and when the window lapses.
- **Pay It Forward** — Two vanilla criteria ANDed. 'handed_over' is thrown_item_picked_up_by_player on a Totem of Undying whose thrower is a player at distance at least 1 — the thrower of your own drop is always at distance 0 from you, so this is self-excluding exactly as fair_trade's 'got' is. 'burned_it' is used_totem, which only ever fires on a blow that would have killed you, so the spare's 'below two hearts' clause is enforced by the game itself rather than by a health read. No detector. The two criteria latch independently and are not time-linked: a totem taken off a teammate in one session and a different totem burned a week later still completes it, which is the same looseness BACAP accepts everywhere.
- **Pocket Money** — The item predicate on the thrown stack carries a minecraft:bundle_contents sub-predicate whose contains list is copied straight from BACAP's animal/flamboyant_range, so all four supplies must be inside the same bundle at the moment the other player picks it up. The original 'four different items' wording became four named items with counts on purpose: contains is attested in BACAP and Enhanced Discoveries, whereas the CollectionPredicate size field appears in neither, and an unattested field is exactly what I am not willing to guess at. Eight of each is 32 of the bundle's 64 capacity units, so there is room to spare and no risk of the kit not fitting.
- **Pony Express** — Pure criteria, no function. Both halves are Written Book handovers with the distance-min-1 thrower guard, and each carries a player predicate pinning the dimension - the entity_properties + location.dimension shape BACAP uses in challenges/root and end/the_actual_end. DELIBERATE CHANGE: the thousand-block relay through three carriers is not expressible without a function that tags an item stack and accumulates distance, so distance is replaced by a dimension crossing, which no single handover can fake and which is a real journey through a portal. Consequently players drops from three to two: pure criteria can prove that SOMEBODY else was involved, never that two different somebodies were, so a three-carrier chain cannot be distinguished from one partner used twice. Tier lowered from challenge to goal to match what is actually being asked.
- **Raid Party** — Every 5 s the detector reads Hero of the Village straight off every non-spectator player's active_effects and grants when three carry it at once. Reading NBT rather than a minecraft:location criterion removes the phase dependence: location fires on each player's own 20-tick cycle, and tagging from it while revoking in the same pass made the count depend on whose cycle happened to align with the sampling pass.
- **Required Reading** — Two criteria ANDed in separate requirement groups: taking a signed written_book off another player's drop, and using a written_book on a lectern block, the latter written with the location_check plus match_tool pair BACAP uses in adventure/im_not_lost_anymore rather than a bare item field. The original framing wanted the book's author component compared against the placing player, which criteria cannot do — a component predicate can only match a fixed value, never 'not you' — so authorship is carried by the handover instead. The two legs are not time-linked and the pack cannot prove the book on the lectern is the same one you were given; the description claims only what is checked.
- **Reunion** — Every 5 s each player's isolation streak advances if no other non-spectator player is within 5000 blocks and is wiped to zero the instant one is. Ten minutes of streak (120 cycles) earns a permanent ticket; two ticket holders standing within 3 blocks are reunited. The streak, not a sticky tag, is what makes it honest: hopping through a Nether portal and walking back only earns a ticket if you actually stay away for ten minutes, so the fact that a distance selector cannot see across dimensions no longer buys anything. The ticket is never taken back, so a veteran can be the other half of a newcomer's reunion, and the streak resets at 120 so nothing grows without bound.
- **Schadenfreude** — SUBSTITUTION for Undertaker, which is impossible in vanilla for a concrete reason: death drops carry no thrower. Needs three players online, counted with `store result ... if entity`, or it returns after two commands.
- **Starter Kit** — Three thrown_item_picked_up_by_player criteria latch when another player's dropped sword, pickaxe and food are collected; entity is entity_type minecraft:player with distance.absolute.min 1, and for this trigger 'entity' is the THROWER, so a self-drop puts the thrower at distance 0 and is excluded outright. DELIBERATE CHANGE from the suggested mechanism: the 'all twenty-seven slots filled' clause was dropped because it is not expressible. - **Always the Passenger** — SUBSTITUTION for Self-Service, which has no multiplayer half in vanilla: stocking a Shelf is item_used_on_block and taking from it is a block use, and neither trigger carries any information about the other player - the pack's own building/trading_post gets round that only by making the third criterion an emerald hand-off. Self-Service also sits between three shipped entries (BACAP Shelfception and Locked and Loaded, this pack's Trading Post and Library Card). Instead this is a collection built from the one shape criteria do prove, in the tab this pack already files shared mounts under (animal/carpool, animal/two_hump_transit). Three started_riding criteria in three AND-ed requirement groups, each with the BACAP player/vehicle entity_properties shape used 25 times across BACAP: the vehicle's FIRST passenger must be a player at a distance greater than zero. Because started_riding fires as you board, and the first passenger is whoever boarded first, the criterion can only latch if you are the one climbing on second - which is exactly the description, and the same semantics as this pack's nether/changing_of_the_guard. Guards differ per vehicle on purpose: 0.3 for Boat and Camel because boat seats are well under a block apart (the value this pack already uses in crows_nest and butch_and_sundance), 1 for the Happy Ghast to stay consistent with changing_of_the_guard. Self always measures exactly 0, so every one of them excludes the driver. Deliberately no Minecart criterion - a Minecart seats one. The three criteria latch independently and never expire, so the three rides can be days apart and with different partners.
- **Caravan of Love** — Reframed from the original leash-chain idea, which is not detectable: a llama caravan lives in the LlamaEntity caravanHead field and is never written to NBT (that is why caravans break on world reload), and BACAP's own 'You Lead, I'll Follow' checks nothing more than a lead used on a llama. So this ANDs that exact BACAP criterion (player_interacted_with_entity with a lead on #blazeandcave:llamas) with taking a Lead off another player's drop, using the same distance-min-1 thrower guard. What the pack can actually see is 'another player supplied the lead you hitched a llama with'; it cannot see whose caravan the llama joined, and the description is written to claim only slightly more than that, in line with the BACAP advancement it hangs off.
- **Carpool** — For each player still missing it, the detector walks 'execute on vehicle ... on passengers' and counts how many of that vehicle's passengers are non-spectator players; if the vehicle is in #blazeandcave:all_boats and the count is two or more, every player aboard is granted. A pure started_riding criterion cannot express this because its vehicle.passenger predicate also matches the mounting player themselves, and players are never written into a vehicle's Passengers NBT, so the passenger walk is the only reliable count. Per-rider counting is correct here because the requirement is two riders in the same boat.
- **Cavalry Charge** — Run at a player who lacks it: every non-spectator within 15 blocks is stepped onto their vehicle, and each vehicle that is a minecraft:horse inside 15 blocks is tagged. The count is then of TAGGED HORSES, not of riders, so the threshold cannot be met by stacking riders on one animal even if a future mount gains extra seats; at four or more distinct mounted horses the passengers of those horses are granted. The tag is cleared before and after the count, and both clears are radius-limited to the same 15 blocks, which is exact because no time passes inside one function execution.
- **Good Boy, Someone Else's** — Pure criteria. 'patched_him_up' is copied verbatim from the vanilla advancement minecraft:husbandry/repair_wolf_armor as BACAP ships it - player_interacted_with_entity, item armadillo_scute, entity a wolf whose body equipment is wolf_armor with minecraft:damage 0 - so it is guaranteed to behave exactly as 'Good as New' does. 'handed_the_scutes' is the distance-min-1 thrower guard on a scute pickup. Honest limitation, and the reason the title moved from the dog to the scutes: an entity predicate has no owner field and there is no non-macro way to compare a wolf's Owner UUID to the acting player, so the pack cannot see whose dog it is. What it can see is that the scutes came out of another player's hands, which is what the description now claims.
- **Guard Dog** — Pure criteria. 'befriended' is BACAP's own Leader of the Pack criterion, character for character - minecraft:tame_animal with entity_type minecraft:wolf. 'handed_the_bones' is the standard distance-min-1 thrower guard on a Bone pickup. Substitution stated plainly: the original wanted a mob killed by a wolf whose owner is a different player while that mob was targeting you, and no vanilla trigger fires on a player for a kill their pet made - player_killed_entity requires the player to land the blow - so the borrowed-pet act is not observable at all. Taming the dog on somebody else's bones is the nearest thing the criteria layer can actually witness, and it keeps the tab, the tier and the two-player requirement.
- **Hog Wild** — minecraft:location is a periodic trigger — vanilla fires it for every player every 20 ticks — so it works as a free once-a-second state check with no detection function at all. The check is: my vehicle is a pig, and the entity my crosshair raycast lands on is a player whose own vehicle is a pig, no more than 5 blocks away. looking_at cannot resolve to yourself, so the second pig-rider is necessarily somebody else. A pig can only be mounted when saddled, so 'saddled' needs no separate test. Substitution note: the original asked for 100 blocks travelled side by side; distance-travelled state cannot be held in criteria, so the advancement asks for the formation instead of the mileage — riding abreast and glancing over is exactly the pose it was describing.
- **Horse Trading** — Both ends of the handover carry a vehicle predicate of entity_type minecraft:horse — the receiver through player, the thrower through entity — using the same vehicle field BACAP reads in monsters/absolute_cinema, so the criterion only fires when two mounted riders are involved. Taken from spares in place of Fifty-Fifty and reframed: the original wanted each rider on the horse the OTHER tamed, which needs the horse's Owner UUID compared against a player at runtime and so needs a function. Riders on adjacent horses sit comfortably past the distance min 1 self-exclusion guard; if they are squeezed closer than that the criterion simply waits until they step apart.
- **Pack Leaders** — Two radii, both stated: wolves are searched and tagged within 24 blocks of the running player, and the qualifying players are the non-spectators within 10 blocks of them. Wolves within 24 blocks are tested with 'if items entity @s armor.body minecraft:wolf_armor' and tagged bacaptmh_armored. Each candidate player then CLAIMS one armoured wolf within 8 blocks that no one else has claimed: if such a wolf exists the player is tagged bacaptmh_leader and the nearest free wolf is tagged bacaptmh_claimed, so one shared wolf can satisfy exactly one player. Three leaders are therefore three players each with their own armoured wolf; the separate 'three wolves in the area' count is gone because the claim already implies it. Wolf Armor can only be equipped on a tamed wolf, so the armour test doubles as the tamed test; ownership is inferred from the wolf sticking near its player rather than from the Owner UUID, which commands cannot compare. All three tags are cleared at both ends of the function.
- **Passing the Lead** — 'handed' fires when you pick up a Lead that another player threw (thrown_item_picked_up_by_player, item lead, thrower predicate entity_type player + distance min 1, so your own dropped lead cannot satisfy it). The original idea wanted the SECOND player to leash the SAME animal the first was leading; proving mob identity needs a detector and the budget was spent elsewhere, so what is checked is 'his lead, your leash'.

Caveat stated plainly: horse_one_cm covers horses, donkeys and mules but not camels or striders, so this is a horseback advancement.
- **Pit Crew** — minecraft:player_interacted_with_entity with a horse-food item and an entity predicate whose `passenger` is a player - the same nested-passenger shape BACAP uses in monsters/lancelot and nether/giddy_up. The rider is required to be at least one block from the feeder, and nested predicates are matched against the same context origin (the feeder's position), so feeding the horse you are yourself riding scores 0 and is excluded. 'Back to full health' is dropped - entity health is not expressible in an entity predicate and a detector was not worth a task.
- **Release the Hounds** — DETECTOR 1 of 5. Vanilla gives the kill credit for a pet kill to the wolf, not its owner, so the only trigger that fires is entity_killed_player on the VICTIM (criterion `mauled`, matching killer entity_type wolf); the impossible guard is ANDed in a separate requirements group so that flag alone can never complete the advancement. bucket_fast runs detect/release_the_hounds as the flagged victim `at @s` — the datapack tick tag runs at the end of the same server tick the death was processed in, before any respawn packet, so @s is still standing on the death spot. It revokes the flag (consuming it), tags itself bacaptmh_mauled, then walks @e[type=wolf,distance=..24,limit=12,sort=nearest] `on owner` and grants to every owner that is a non-spectator player and is not the victim. Known weakness: the wolf that landed the blow is not identified, so a wild-wolf kill next to somebody else's tamed wolf would credit that owner; and `execute on owner` is not attested anywhere in the installed packs, only in vanilla command grammar.
- **Reservoir Dogs** — Function-granted by detect/reservoir_dogs, dispatched every medium tick as each non-spectator missing it who has any Wolf within 24 blocks. Wolves linger at Health 0.0f through a ~20-tick death animation, so a 10-tick sampler always catches at least two frames of it. The detector examines exactly ONE dying wolf per pass (limit=1,sort=nearest), so the dead wolf's owner and the killer's owner can never be read out of two different fights: 'execute as <dying wolf> on owner' tags the loser, and 'on attacker if entity @s[type=wolf] on owner' tags the winner - the vanilla attacker relation resolves to the entity that last damaged the wolf, so a wolf killed by a player, a mob or fall damage yields no winner tag at all. The grant needs a loser tag to exist and goes only to a winner-tagged player who is not also loser-tagged, which is what enforces 'two different owners, both online players'. Deliberately one-sided: the losing owner is not granted, since the advancement says win. Caveat I am not fully certain of: the attacker relation reflects the last thing that hurt the wolf, so a wolf softened by another player's wolf and finished by something else would not count, and a wolf finished by another player's wolf after being softened by anything else would.
- **Sting Operation** — Two criteria that can never both belong to the same player: 'angered' fires for whoever hits a bee (player_hurt_entity, the same shape as BACAP's mob-hurt entries, with the distance min 1 guard), 'stung' fires for whoever a bee kills (entity_killed_player with entity_type bee, exactly the shape BACAP's Endergeddon uses). detect/sting_operation stamps the angerer with a 30-second bacaptmh_stt window and releases their flag so the next bee re-arms it; when a stung player is processed it grants to every OTHER non-spectator within 64 blocks still inside that window, then to the victim, and only releases the stung flag if nobody was in range. The distance=1..64 selector excludes the victim themselves, so a lone player who angers their own hive and dies to it gets nothing. Both grants happen before the stung flag is released, so a completed advancement is never un-completed by the revoke.
- **Two-Hump Transit** — Identical passenger walk to Carpool but the vehicle must be a minecraft:camel; when one camel carries two non-spectator player passengers both riders are granted.

<details><summary>How these are detected</summary>

- **Farm Share** — An AND of two pickup criteria: a full stack of Bread of yours picked up by another player, and a full stack of wheat, carrots, potatoes or beetroot of theirs picked up by you, both with the one-block self-exclusion. The 9x9 split field in the original idea needed per-block placement ownership and a maturity sweep - far beyond one detection function - so the advancement was rebuilt around the same fiction, two farmers trading their harvests, in pure criteria. The two halves need not be the same trade.
- **Golden Handshake** — Pure criteria, no detector. 'handed' is minecraft:thrown_item_picked_up_by_player - the trigger fires for the player who PICKS UP an item another entity threw, and its entity field is the thrower (BACAP's adventure/awards_ceremony uses exactly this shape with an Allay as the thrower). Pinning the thrower to entity_type player with distance min 1 is the diamonds_to_you self-exclusion idiom: a player who drops his own apple and walks back to it measures the distance from himself to himself, always 0, so he can never serve himself. 'eaten' is consume_item on the same item with an entity_scores check on bacaptmh_hp, a vanilla health objective, at 4 or below - two hearts. Requirements default to AND of both criteria. It is not verified that the apple eaten is the same apple that was handed over; both halves are required, and the receiving half is impossible alone.
- **Group Therapy** — Function-granted (detector 1 of 3). bucket_medium counts non-spectators into its own #gtn score — counted, never `if entity @a[limit=3]` — and only then enters detect/group_therapy, and only while somebody still lacks it. Eating a Golden Apple raises minecraft.used:minecraft.golden_apple; the detector compares that statistic against a private mirror, so a rise means an apple was eaten in the last 10 ticks. The health test is deliberately NOT read at detection time: a Golden Apple grants Absorption the instant it is swallowed and the vanilla health criterion counts absorption, so the eater would already read back above three hearts. Instead an ungated bucket line stamps bacaptmh_glow=4 on anyone currently at 5 health or less, and the detector requires that stamp to still be alive. A qualifying eater gets a 6-window (60 ticks); when three or more players hold a live window at once, all of them are granted. The mirror refresh, the low-health stamp and both window countdowns are ungated bucket lines that run whether or not the detector did, and the mirror is also seeded in seed_lines, so a lifetime apple total can never be read as a fresh event and a window can never freeze while the gate is shut. Known limit: proximity is not checked, so the three could be in different dimensions — the coordination is in the three-second window, not the geography.
- **Have Your Cake** — The 'bite' criterion is not invented: BACAP's own technical/castaway_fail.json detects a cake slice being eaten with exactly this structure - minecraft:default_block_use plus a location_check on minecraft:cake - alongside its consume_item criteria for every other food, so eating cake is proven to route through the empty-hand block-use path. An impossible guard sits in its own requirements group, so the latch alone never completes the advancement. Every medium tick the cake window is aged first, then anyone holding the latch runs detect/have_your_cake, which revokes the latch before anything else, opens a thirty-second window (60 medium ticks) on itself, and counts players whose own window is still open within six blocks using 'execute store result ... if entity' (a match COUNT, not the at-least-one test 'if entity @a[limit=4]' would give). At four it grants to all of them, not only whoever took the last slice. Six blocks is one player's reach on a cake plus slack, so it reads as one cake rather than a room of them; it cannot literally compare block positions, and two cakes side by side would also pass. gamemode=!creative is on both selectors because Player.canEat returns true for an invulnerable player, so a creative player can in fact eat cake.
- **Piece of Cake** — Pure criteria, no detector. The give/take pair is TMH adventure/fair_trade's exact shape, narrowed to minecraft:cake on both sides, so it is strictly harder than Fair Trade rather than a duplicate of it. Both guards pin the other party to entity_type player at distance min 1, which excludes drop-and-repick. Requirements default to AND, and there is no timing window - Fair Trade already owns the five-second swap. Reworded from 'two players each eat a slice of the same Cake': eating cake is a block interaction with no per-block sharing signal, so the original needed a detector watching bite states.
- **Potluck** — One detector (detect/m1_potluck). 'A different food' is expressed as five disjoint food families, each with its own consume_item criterion; the medium bucket turns each latch into its own 20-medium-tick (ten second) window plus a shared 'somebody ate' window, then revokes the latch, so nothing here reads a lifetime eating statistic. The detector runs at any player with a live shared window who lacks the advancement: it counts how many of the five family windows are alive within eight blocks, returns early unless all five are, then counts distinct eaters with `execute store result ... run execute if entity` and grants to all of them at five. requirements are an OR group over the five foods AND-ed with an impossible group, so the five criteria alone can never complete it. Known edge: five families and five eaters in range is checked, not a bijection, so one player who ate three different families alongside four others who ate the remaining two would also qualify.
- **Seed Exchange** — Pure criteria, no function - the shape of bacaptmh:adventure/fair_trade minus its detector. 'gave' is thrown_item_picked_up_by_entity (you threw it, a player picked it up); 'got' is thrown_item_picked_up_by_player (a player threw it, you picked it up); both carry the diamonds_to_you distance absolute min 1 guard, so dropping and re-collecting your own seeds satisfies neither. Default AND requirements. Deliberate change from the suggested mechanism: the five-second pairing window is dropped, because enforcing it would cost a detector this batch cannot afford. The consequence is honest and small - the give and the receive need not be the same trade, and since both items are in one list the pair could in principle be the same species both ways. Both items are Sniffer-exclusive, so ancient seeds still had to move in both directions between two people.
- **Soup Kitchen** — Seven copies of thrown_item_picked_up_by_player, one per foodLevel from 0 to 6, because entity predicates have no hunger field and NBT matching is exact - the seven sit in one OR group, so any hunger at or below three shanks satisfies it. Each demands a real food item and a thrower who is a player at distance min 1. The second group is a consume_item on the same food list. Deliberately checked at PICKUP time rather than at eating time: it is not settled whether consume_item fires before or after the food is applied, and being starving when the parcel arrives is the unambiguous reading. The eaten item is not proven to be the item you were given.
- **Stew Roulette** — Written from the eater's side so it is expressible as pure criteria: the stew must have been picked up off another player's drop (thrower entity_type player, distance min 1) and then eaten, with consume_item lifted verbatim from BACAP suspicious_looking_stew. No detector. The 'catch the effect' clause is dropped — effects_changed cannot tell that the effect came from a stew as opposed to any other source — but eating a Suspicious Stew always applies its effect anyway, so nothing is lost in practice. Only the eater is granted; the cook is not, which matches how criteria-only entries such as First Blood behave.

</details>

<details><summary>How these are detected</summary>

- **Bring a Torch** — One criterion, no detector. The pickup half is the fair_trade 'got' shape — thrower entity_type player at distance at least 1, which self-excludes because the thrower of your own drop is measured against itself and is always at distance 0. The darkness half is the light predicate straight out of BACAP mining/heart_of_darkness, nested in the generic 'player' entity predicate that every criterion accepts. Light 0 is the combined block-and-sky brightness, so an open surface at midnight (moonlight leaves it at about 4) does not qualify; a cave or a sealed room does. Being a single criterion, requirements is left empty and the default all-criteria rule applies.
- **Deep Recovery** — Pure criteria, AND-ed, no detector. A player's death drops carry that player as their Thrower, so picking one up fires thrown_item_picked_up_by_player with `entity` bound to the dead owner - which is why entity_type player plus distance min 1 is enough to mean 'somebody else's belongings' and can never be your own drop. The y-bound uses the exact location/position shape BACAP uses in end/the_void_frogs and end/uh_houston_we_have_a_problem. The return leg is the mirrored trigger (thrown_item_picked_up_by_entity, the diamonds_to_you idiom) fired for the giver with the picker pinned to a player at least a block away, above Y=0. Stated honestly: the trigger cannot tell a death drop from a deliberate toss, and nothing checks that the items handed back are the same items or that the recipient is the same player - what is enforced is a genuine two-player exchange, one leg below Y=-50 and one above ground.
- **Dig Me Out** — Detector bacaptmh:detect/dig_me_out, called unconditionally from bucket_medium. Its first three lines keep a private mirror of every non-spectator's minecraft.mined:minecraft.gravel and turn any increase into a 3-window (1.5 s) bacaptmh_gravt flag; they are deliberately ungated so a mirror can never freeze while somebody is offline and then read a lifetime total as a fresh dig, and the mirror itself is created in bacaptmh:seed. The state machine then runs as/at each survival player who still lacks the advancement: while the block at ~ ~1 ~ is gravel and another player is within 6 blocks it stamps bacaptmh_bury 3; when that head block is gone, the victim's own gravel counter did NOT move, and a different player within 6 blocks has a live gravel flag, both the victim and every qualifying digger are granted. Requiring the victim's own gravel delta to be zero is what stops someone from digging themselves out and claiming a rescue. Attribution is by proximity plus mined-gravel statistic rather than a real block-break hook, so a bystander who happened to be mining gravel a few blocks away in the same 1.5 s could be credited alongside the real digger.
- **Homing Pigeon** — 'handed' is a Compass picked up from another player's throw. 'arrived' is the exact criterion of vanilla adventure/use_lodestone (item_used_on_block with a location_check on lodestone and a match_tool on compass) - you have to be standing at the Lodestone and right-click it, which is the arrival proof. Both are required. The original idea stored the Lodestone's coordinates and watched the recipient close on them; that needed a detector, so instead the recipient's own use of the compass ON the Lodestone stands in for reaching it. Nothing forces the Lodestone to be the one the compass was locked to, or the one the giver placed.
- **Loan Shark** — Three AND'd criteria: a Diamond Pickaxe of yours picked up by another player, a Diamond Pickaxe of theirs picked up by you, and a durability change on a Diamond Pickaxe leaving it at 780 or less of its 1561 - the `durability` field of item_durability_changed, used as a bare int in BACAP's end/some_breakthrough and taken here as a bounded range. Stamping the actual item with the borrower's wear would have needed custom_data and a detector; this keeps the story (it went out, it came back, it is half dead) without tracking item identity, so a player who lends one pickaxe and is handed back a different one still qualifies.
- **Outsourced** — Pure criteria, AND-ed. 'diamonds' needs a single dropped stack of three or more diamonds picked up from another player — item.count is the ItemPredicate field BACAP uses in end/what_for, and because 'entity' on this trigger is the thrower, a self-drop sits at distance 0 and is excluded. 'pickaxe' is minecraft:recipe_crafted on recipe_id minecraft:diamond_pickaxe, the shape BACAP uses in monsters/boned. Not enforced: that the three diamonds you were given are the three that went into the pickaxe.
- **Passing the Torch** — Pure criteria, AND of two groups. 'handed' is thrown_item_picked_up_by_player on minecraft:copper_torch with entity_type player and the distance min 1 self-exclusion guard, so the torch must come out of somebody else's hand. 'placed' is the placed_block block-predicate shape copied verbatim from blazeandcave:building/chartreuse, including both block forms a placed copper torch can take (copper_torch standing, copper_wall_torch on a side). Both criteria land on the receiver, so this is the receiver's advancement; the ten-second window from the original idea is not enforced, because criteria carry no timing relationship, and the description no longer claims one.
- **Silent Duel** — Pure criteria, no function - the suggested shrieker-counting detector is replaced by its observable consequence. A Sculk Shrieker's activation is what applies Darkness to every player within 40 blocks for 12 seconds, so 'neither of us has Darkness at the moment of the kill' is the criteria-expressible form of 'nobody set a Shrieker off'. Both the killer (player) and the victim (entity) are given as lists of loot conditions: an entity_properties term pinning location.biomes to minecraft:deep_dark (the field shape verified against monsters/warden_frostbite), ANDed with a minecraft:inverted term over an effects predicate for minecraft:darkness (the inverted/effects shapes verified against enchanting/silent_but_deadly and end/dogfight). No self-exclusion guard is needed: player_killed_entity fires for the killer and a player cannot be their own killed entity. A nearby Warden also applies Darkness and would block it - which is correct, since a Warden means Shriekers went off.
- **Split Shift** — Pure criteria, no detector, one criterion so no custom requirements are needed. thrown_item_picked_up_by_entity fires on the GIVER when the stack is picked up; `count: 64` is the exact-integer item-predicate form BACAP uses in enchanting/magical_stockpile, and a dropped full stack arrives as one 64-count item entity. The picker is pinned to entity_type player with the distance min 1 self-exclusion, so picking your own drop back up does nothing. Reframed from the original 'fuel a Blast Furnace, let someone pull 64 ingots out of it': no vanilla trigger reports furnace fuelling or output extraction, so that version would have cost a container-polling detector for a goal-tier entry.
- **Tunnel Vision** — Detector, bucket_medium. There is no aggregate 'blocks mined' statistic, so mined:stone + mined:deepslate + mined:tuff are summed by hand and differenced against a mirror seeded in bacaptmh:seed; any window delta over 200 is discarded as a stale mirror rather than trusted as a burst of digging. The first block of a dig records that dig head's x/z, and ten seconds with nothing broken ends the dig and zeroes its tally, so the 150-block threshold means 150 blocks in one continuous session. When a player is below y=50 with 150 in the current dig and the nearest other survival player within three blocks also has 150, the squared horizontal distance between the two dig heads is computed - both axis deltas clamped to 1000 before squaring so the multiply cannot overflow a 32-bit score - and both are granted at 200 blocks or more. What is enforced is where each tunnel started and where the two of them met, not that the last block broken was literally the wall between them.

</details>

<details><summary>How these are detected</summary>

- **Assist** — Every 0.5 s each player whose damage_dealt delta is positive stamps bacaptmh_dmgt with the gametime and walks the monsters within 12 blocks. The walk is #blazeandcave:hostile_monsters MINUS the three bosses that tag also contains (Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden): the tag keeps armour stands, boats, item frames, villagers and farm animals out of the search, and the three exclusions keep Assist about ordinary mobs. Without them this goal would have been a free side effect of Tag Team, Wither Committee and Warden Party in this same batch - a boss lingers at 0 health through a long death animation, necessarily passes through 0.1-3.9 HP under a 10-tick sampler, and those three advancements already guarantee two or more co-located players with fresh damage deltas in range - and 'beaten down below two hearts' reads absurdly for a 200-HP dragon. A monster read between 0.1 and 3.9 health gets the entity tag bacaptmh_softened, an entity tag rather than a scoreboard row, so it vanishes with the mob and nothing can leak. When a monster carrying that tag is next seen at 0 health, the finisher's own sample counts how many non-spectator, non-creative players within 16 blocks dealt damage in the last 100 ticks; at two or more, all of them are granted and the tag is removed. The health read is now a single data get, with the shared temp score reset to a sentinel first instead of an 'unless data entity @s Health' probe, so the walk costs one NBT access per mob per damaging player rather than two while still being safe against a read that returns nothing. There is no per-mob identity scoreboard at all, so the pid/soft/softby objectives and their permanent UUID rows are gone. Attribution is still by statistic delta plus proximity rather than by a damage-source hook, so in a dense melee a second attacker who was hitting a different mob nearby can be credited alongside the real pair.
- **Avenged** — One pure criterion, and the only thing in the batch that reads another player without touching an item. The entity predicate's targeted_entity field walks the mob's current attack target - Enhanced Discoveries chains it three deep in adventure/save_me.json and monsters/suspicious_duel.json - and a nested entity predicate is evaluated against the SAME origin as its parent, i.e. the killing player's position. So targeted_entity of entity_type minecraft:player at distance min 8 means 'the monster's target is a player standing at least eight blocks from me', which is somebody else by construction, and puts the monster itself out at that range too. killing_blow with the minecraft:is_projectile tag is BACAP weaponry/poseidon_vs_hades' idiom and makes it a shot rather than a scuffle. The tag #blazeandcave:hostile_monsters ships with BACAP and is used the same way in weaponry/indiana_jones.json. One caveat I have not hidden: this assumes the mob's target field is still populated when PLAYER_KILLED_ENTITY fires from awardKillScore during death - nothing clears it on death, so it should hold, but it is the single assumption here I would want seen once on a server. Distinct from Too Many Hands 'Assist', which is about finishing a mob a teammate wounded; this is about the mob still hunting them.
- **Bait and Switch** — DETECTOR 3 of 5. `looted` is player_generates_container_loot on minecraft:chests/ancient_city, the exact shape BACAP challenges/i_am_loot uses for every other chest table; the impossible criterion is a separate requirements group so the loot flag alone can never finish it. bucket_fast runs detect/bait_and_switch as any player holding {looted=true,impossible=false}. Because a Warden's target is null for whole ticks at a time, the flag is NOT consumed on the first pass: bacaptmh_baitw counts the passes and only revokes after 200 of them (ten seconds of grace). Each pass walks @e[type=warden,distance=..96,limit=4,sort=nearest] `on target at @s`; the target qualifies if it is a non-spectator player other than the looter and the looter is 30 or more blocks away, at which point both are granted. Honest limitation: `execute on target` is vanilla grammar but is not attested in any installed pack, and a Warden that has just lost aggro reads as no target at all.
- **Bomb Disposal** — Detector, bucket_medium, dispatched straight onto the Creepers within 12 blocks of any player who lacks it (limit=6, sort=nearest, so the selector is bounded). It reads the Creeper's Health and does nothing unless it is zero - that is the whole trick, because a Creeper that detonates is discarded outright with its health intact, so zero health only ever means it was killed, and a killed mob lingers through a 20-tick death animation that a 10-tick sampler cannot miss. It then requires a player inside 3.5 blocks, the range at which a Creeper is actually swelling, and two or more non-spectators within 12 blocks, and grants all of them - the saved and the saviour alike, since Cooperative Mode is off. The fuse is inferred from that proximity: swelling is not persisted in NBT (only flint-and-steel ignition sets 'ignited'), so there is no way to read a natural hiss directly.
- **Bounty Hunter** — Two vanilla criteria ANDed: picking Emeralds off another player's drop with the distance-min-1 thrower guard, and a player_killed_entity on the existing #blazeandcave:hostile_monsters entity tag. No detector, and therefore no five-second link between the kill and the payment — the two latch independently, so in practice this reads as 'be paid in Emeralds by a teammate at some point after you have killed a monster'. That is deliberate: tying the two together would have cost a detector for a task-tier entry, and the multiplayer requirement lives entirely in the payment half, which no solo player can produce.
- **Death Do Us Part** — The death itself is a pure criterion - entity_killed_player with entity_type creeper, exactly BACAP monsters/aww_man - and requirements are [[creeper],[impossible]], an AND of groups, so it never completes on its own. bucket_fast runs every tick: it first decays bacaptmh_dup, then stamps 6 on anyone carrying the flag and revokes the flag immediately, so the criterion can never latch and re-fire. detect/death_pair runs at each stamped player and grants to every stamped player within 16 blocks once a real head count finds two or more. One shared creeper is approximated by 'two creeper deaths inside six ticks and sixteen blocks' rather than by comparing the creeper's UUID, which commands cannot do; with doImmediateRespawn on both victims are at spawn together, which the same test still accepts.
- **Guardian Angel** — One criterion, no function. targeted_entity is the field Enhanced Discoveries uses in Save Me! and Suspicious Duel, so the mob's current attack target can be tested directly: it must be a minecraft:player, and distance is measured from the killer's own position, so a mob hunting the killer sits at distance 0 and is excluded by min 1 - the target has to be somebody else. Restricted to #blazeandcave:hostile_monsters so it cannot be satisfied by a tamed wolf or an iron golem. Stated uncertainty: this relies on the mob still holding its target when player_killed_entity fires during LivingEntity.die. I believe it does, because the trigger runs before the entity is discarded, but I have not been able to confirm it from the reference packs, and if the target is cleared first the advancement would simply never fire rather than fire wrongly. The 'below three hearts' clause of the original idea was dropped: no attested entity predicate exposes health, so it would have cost a detection function.
- **Heads Up** — One criterion, the giver's side of the handoff: thrown_item_picked_up_by_entity fires on the thrower with 'this' bound to whoever picked the drop up, which is exactly how BACAP technical/give_piglin_gold detects a piglin taking your gold. The picker is pinned to a player at distance min 1, the diamonds_to_you guard - your own drop is always at distance 0 from you, so a projectile-style self-pickup cannot satisfy it, and a mob cannot either. minecraft:player_head is deliberately NOT in the list: building/trophy_wall already covers passing a player's own head around, and leaving it out keeps this strictly about mob heads. No detector.
- **Heart Surgery** — Pure criteria, no function — and the reason it works is BACAP itself: blazeandcave:biomes/heart_breaker ('Smash a Creaking Heart to destroy its Creaking') is implemented as player_killed_entity on entity_type creaking, which proves the breaker is credited with the Creaking's death. This adds the role split with targeted_entity — the same nested field Enhanced Discoveries uses in Save Me! and Dinner Time — requiring the dying Creaking's target to be a player at least 4 blocks from the breaker. Nested distance is measured from the same origin as the outer predicate (the breaker's position), so a target 4+ blocks away cannot be the breaker himself; alone, the Creaking would be hunting you and the check fails. Stated uncertainty: this assumes the Creaking's target is still set on the tick it is torn down by the heart breaking, which I could not verify from the shipped packs.
- **Hold the Line** — No detector; two vanilla criteria paired in the medium bucket. `blocked` is BACAP monsters/blast_shield's damage predicate with the creeper swapped for a Ravager; when it latches, the bucket opens a 10-medium-tick (five second) window and immediately revokes the hook, so a block survived last week cannot be paired with today's kill. `slew` latches on the finisher. The grant lines run `at` the finisher and target @a[distance=1..24,...] with a live block window - distance=1..24 excludes the finisher himself, so one player who both blocked and killed gets nothing. The tank is granted first, then the finisher, then the `slew` hook is revoked; the revoke line is gated on impossible=false so it can never un-complete a finished advancement. requirements are three AND-ed groups, one of them impossible, so the criteria alone never complete it. Honest limit: identity of the Ravager is not tracked - the pairing is proximity (24 blocks) plus the five-second window, so in a raid with two Ravagers the block and the kill could in principle be on different animals.
- **Human Shield** — minecraft:player_killed_entity on a Warden whose `targeted_entity` is a player at least four blocks from the killer; a Warden aggroed on the killer measures 0 and is excluded, so somebody else must be holding its attention. The sonic-boom geometry in the original idea is not expressible - a boom only ever damages the Warden's current target, so 'take the hit meant for someone else' cannot be detected - and the ray cast it would need was not worth a detection function. Honest caveat: this pack's existing Warden Party is likely to be earned in the same fight, since three players around a Warden usually means it is looking at one of the other two.
- **Last Stand** — 'slew' arms the check and the impossible guard, in its own requirement group, stops it completing on criteria alone. detect/last_stand then runs every tick for the killer and reads bacaptmh_dtime, the per-player death stamp detect/sample keeps, against #f3cut1200 (the 60-second cutoff computed in the medium bucket, so up to nine ticks stale here). It grants when at least two OTHER players died inside that minute and every player within 64 blocks of the killer is one of them - i.e. nobody is left standing beside him. The fallen are granted too. 'Joined the fight' is approximated by 'died in the last minute'; a teammate who died elsewhere in the world at the same time would also count.
- **Night Watch** — Criteria latch the two halves, a detector pairs them. requirements is an AND of three single-criterion groups including the impossible 'guard', so no lone player can ever complete it from criteria — that grouping is deliberate, one group containing all three would be an OR and would defeat the guard. bacaptmh:detect/night_watch runs from bucket_medium whenever 2 or more players are online and at least one still lacks it: it stamps a 20-window (10 s) timer on anyone holding 'slept' or 'killed' and immediately revokes that criterion, so neither half can sit latched for days waiting for the other. Any sleeper with a live timer who has a player with a live kill timer within 1..10 blocks grants to both. #blazeandcave:hostile_monsters is the same entity tag detect/assist walks, and entity_type accepts a tag exactly as blazeandcave:monsters/keep_your_distance does. The sleeper is identified by the slept_in_bed trigger rather than by live sleeping state, which I could not test for without an NBT field I had no shipped example of; in practice the 10 s window covers the night skip.
- **Noble Sacrifice** — Half criteria, half detector. 'fell' is entity_killed_player with entity_type wither — the same shape BACAP uses for the Warden — and the impossible guard sits in its own requirements group so the flag alone never completes it. detect/noble_sacrifice consumes 'fell' with an advancement revoke (the field_medic idiom) and converts it into a 120-window (60 s), so a death from months ago can never be paid out by an unrelated Wither kill. The avenger is whoever's minecraft.killed:minecraft.wither rose in that window; he is tagged and excluded from the payout, so dying to your own Wither and then finishing it yourself is rejected. Only the fallen are granted — deliberately, since the advancement describes the dead player's role and granting the avenger would reopen the solo path.
- **Pincer Movement** — Function-granted by detect/pincer, dispatched every medium tick (gated on two or more online, and on at least one player still missing it) as each non-spectator, non-creative player whose bacaptmh_dmgd delta is positive in this window - the same damage-statistic delta detect/sample already maintains for Assist, which is ungated and self-seeding, so an arriving player's lifetime damage_dealt can never be mistaken for a hit. The detector considers only the single nearest hostile monster within 4.5 blocks, so two attackers can never be matched across two different mobs, tags every fresh damager within 4.5 blocks of that mob, and grants to each tagged player who has another tagged player at least 7 blocks away. 'Opposite sides' is that geometry rather than a yaw or dot-product calculation: both attackers inside 4.5 blocks of the mob while at least 7 blocks apart puts the mob between them at an angle of roughly 100 degrees or more. Attribution is by damage-statistic delta plus proximity, not by a damage-source hook, so in a dense melee a player who hit a different mob in the same half second can be credited; the tag is cleared at the end of every pass.
- **Taking One For The Team** — Function-granted, in three phases. MARK: every medium tick each eligible player's Health is read once at scale 10, and anyone reading 1..10 (0.1 to 1.0 HP, i.e. half a heart or less, with a dead player's true 0 excluded) writes their own player id and the gametime onto every eligible player within 8 blocks, in two single commands. Marking from the LIVING player's position is the point: nothing is ever measured from where a corpse happens to be, so this works with doImmediateRespawn on. OPEN: a player whose death stamp and whose mark are both under 100 ticks old, and whose death has not already been processed, opens a 120-window (60 s) watch on the ally named by the stored player id - matched by score, never by substituting a name into a selector. WATCH: the watch is cancelled outright if the ally picks up a fresh death stamp. PAY: when it runs out, the advancement is granted to the survivor AND, by player id, to the player who went down beside them - Cooperative Mode is off, so both participants are paid. Known softness: the mark is whoever was last on half a heart within 8 blocks in that window, so in a crowded fight with two dying players the credited pair can be the wrong one of two adjacent candidates.
- **Total Party Kill** — Deaths are detected from a vanilla deathCount objective (bacaptmh_deaths) that detect/sample reads and immediately resets every window, stamping bacaptmh_dtime with the gametime; sitting on the death screen stamps it too, as a second path. That means the wipe is still detected on servers running doImmediateRespawn, where nobody ever occupies the death screen, and it cannot be missed by a player who respawns between two samples. The check counts online non-spectator, non-creative players and how many of them carry a stamp newer than 200 ticks; when the two counts are equal and there are at least three players, everyone is granted. A counter that is consumed rather than compared against a saved lifetime total cannot produce a first-run false positive.
- **Trial by Fire** — Function-granted. Two UNGATED bucket_medium lines read every non-spectator's Fire NBT into bacaptmh_fire with 'execute store result ... run data get entity @s Fire' — ungated so a player who already holds the advancement still counts towards somebody else's three, and refreshed every pass so no burning state is ever read from a stale score. detect/trial_by_fire then counts burning players within 16 blocks; at three it arms all of them for 20 medium ticks (ten seconds) and stamps their current deathCount into bacaptmh_tbfd. It pays out when three armed players in that radius are all at Fire <= 0 and all still carry the same deathCount they were armed with — that is the 'and live' half, and it is done with a deathCount objective seeded in seed_lines rather than with the shared bacaptmh_dtime, whose score is simply absent for a player who has never died and would have made the advancement unobtainable for them. Grants only to the three who actually burned and survived.
- **Warden Party** — Same shape as Wither Committee but on the Warden at 16 blocks. 'Nobody dead' is enforced with the pack's death mirror rather than with a snapshot of the death screen: bacaptmh_deaths is a vanilla deathCount objective, sampled and consumed every window in detect/sample, which stamps bacaptmh_dtime with the gametime of the player's last death (the Health:0.0f test is kept as a second path). A counted player must be within 16 blocks, not on the death screen, and must have no death stamp inside the last 1200 ticks, so dying at any point in the last minute of the fight disqualifies you for the rest of it. That is enforceable on a 10-tick sampler and works with doImmediateRespawn on, unlike the old 'is on the death screen at this exact sample' test, which a player who died and respawned walked straight through. The Warden's death animation keeps the entity around long enough for the sampler to catch Health 0.
- **Wither Committee** — Gated on three or more non-spectators actually being online (counted properly into #players bacaptmh_sys, not with a limit= selector, which only ever tests for one match). Players who lack the advancement and have a Wither within 20 blocks run a check as the nearest Wither; it reads the Wither's Health NBT and, once Health is 0 (the Wither persists through its long death animation before dropping the Nether Star), counts non-spectator, non-creative players within 20 blocks who are not on the death screen and grants to all of them if there are at least three. Counting is never gated on who already holds the advancement, so a player who already has it still counts toward the three and cannot lock anybody out.

- **The Great Train Robbery** — SUBSTITUTION for Stretcher Case. Stretcher Case needs three things vanilla criteria cannot see: a passenger's health, distance travelled, and who laid the track - all counters or NBT reads, i.e. a function. It is also boxed in on both sides by advancements this pack already ships: redstone/park_and_ride is 'ride a Minecart another player handed you' and challenges/no_man_left_behind is 'under two hearts, take a Golden Apple from another player', so the obvious vanilla rendition (be handed a Golden Apple while sitting in a cart) would have been a mash-up of two existing entries. The distance min 1 guard is the mining/diamonds_to_you idiom: it costs nothing here but closes the freak case of killing yourself with your own TNT or arrow while riding, where the victim entity would be you at distance 0. Nothing checks that the cart was moving; a parked cart counts.
- **Toll Booth** — Pure criteria, no detector. 'booth' copies blazeandcave:redstone/target_acquired's placed_block shape exactly — a location list holding one minecraft:location_check with a block predicate — swapping target for iron_door. 'toll' is the guarded pickup with an item count bound of min 16, the same MinMaxBounds field bacaped:challenges/so_bright uses on an ItemPredicate; the sixteen emeralds must arrive as one stack thrown by another player. Documented reframe: the idea wanted the payer to then walk through the door inside a 200-tick window, which needs a detector and a placement log. That half is roleplay here — the advancement verifies that you built the door and that somebody paid the toll, not that they passed through it.
- **Trapped!** — Opening a trapped chest latches the `opened` criterion, using the same default_block_use + location_check shape BACAP uses in redstone/troll_chest. The detector consumes the criterion, then grants to the opener and to any other player within 32 blocks whose bacaptmh_tct window is still live, i.e. who placed a trapped chest in the last minute.
- **Trust Fall** — A minecraft.used:minecraft.water_bucket delta opens a sixty-second window on whoever pours; only while some window is open does the fast bucket track anyone at all, and then only players who are not on the ground, so the per-tick FallDistance read is bounded. Landing in a minecraft:water block with a peak of 30+, with a windowed pourer other than the faller within 24 blocks, grants both and zeroes the peak. The bank-a-dive exploit is closed: the peak is cleared by ANY water, not just by touching ground, so diving 30 blocks into a natural lake and staying in it no longer leaves a live 30 sitting there waiting for someone to place a bucket nearby. Tracking is no longer gated on the faller lacking the advancement, so a veteran can still act as the faller for a pourer who needs it. Spectators and creative are excluded on both ends.

The detector runs in bucket_fast but is reached only through two cheap gates: a player standing directly on a Hopper, or a player inside the ten-second window that paying opens. It opens that window when an emerald item entity is within two blocks of a customer stood on a Hopper (a dropped emerald falls onto the Hopper and is sucked in), then looks for an item within six blocks lying against a Dispenser on any of its six faces or inside its block space, with no Thrower tag and not an emerald - a thrown item always carries Thrower, a dispensed one never does, which is what stops a hand-dropped decoy. It grants only when a DIFFERENT player (distance=1..32, which excludes the customer at distance 0) holds both build criteria, so nobody can vend to themselves, and it grants shopkeeper and customer together.

<details><summary>How these are detected</summary>

- **Ashes to Ashes** — Detector, one file. Every medium tick, while at least one player still lacks it, detect/ashes_to_ashes runs as/at each survival player: their Y is read only when the execution dimension is the End (hence the bucket line's 'at @s'), and below Y=-32 there is nothing left to stand on, so they are already falling. That stamps a 20-window (20 medium ticks = 200 ticks = ten seconds). The function then counts open windows and grants to every player holding one as soon as there are two. It fires on the plunge rather than on the death itself, which is why no death-cause plumbing is needed.
- **Congregation** — The slow bucket counts non-spectator players who are NOT in minecraft:the_end by iterating and adding, never by an @a[limit=N] test. If that count is zero and at least three non-spectators are online, everyone non-spectator is granted. Spectators are ignored so an AFK observer cannot block it, and the three-player floor stops it firing for a lone player wandering the End. Nobody is excluded from the count for already holding it, so a veteran in the End does not break it for the rest.
- **Eye Contact** — The criterion is copied straight off BACAP's Wax Off: item_used_on_block with a location_check on the block and a match_tool on the item, here end_portal_frame and ender_eye. detect/eye_contact adds one to a lifetime bacaptmh_eye tally, stamps a 30-second bacaptmh_eyt freshness window, releases the criterion so the next eye re-arms it, then counts non-spectators within 12 blocks who have seated at least six eyes AND are still inside their freshness window; at two or more it grants to all of them. The eye tally is a counter this pack increments itself, not a mirrored statistic, so there is no lifetime-total-read-as-an-event hazard. The honest limitation: the tally is lifetime rather than per-portal, so the six-each threshold can be reached across two strongholds - deliberately, because a stronghold portal can spawn with up to three eyes already seated and a strict per-portal six-each would then be unreachable. The freshness window plus the 12-block radius is what makes it 'this portal, together, now'.
- **Ghost Protocol** — Function-granted. Same dragon sampler as detect/tag_team but on its own private stamp objective (bacaptmh_gpt), so it does not go dark once everybody holds Tag Team and that detector stops being dispatched. Every 0.5 s the dragon's Health NBT is read; when it drops, every non-spectator non-creative player within 128 blocks whose damage_dealt delta is positive in the same window is stamped with the gametime. The delta comes from the pack's existing ungated, self-seeding detect/sample. At Health 0 (the dragon lingers through its ~200-tick death animation) the wounders stamped in the last 2400 ticks are counted, and separately the wounders who ALSO carry a death stamp under 2400 ticks old are counted; the advancement is granted to every wounder only when the two counts are equal and there are at least four. 'Died during the fight' is therefore approximated as 'died in the last two minutes', and attribution is by damage-statistic delta plus proximity rather than by a real damage-source hook, exactly as Tag Team already is.
- **Heir Apparent** — One requirements group holding both criteria, so this is deliberately an OR: the player who throws the egg earns it through 'gave' (thrown_item_picked_up_by_entity, the trigger that fires for the thrower, with the picker-up as 'entity') and the player who picks it up earns it through 'got' (thrown_item_picked_up_by_player, which fires for the receiver with the thrower as 'entity'). Both carry entity_type player and distance.absolute.min 1, so dropping the egg and picking it back up yourself is excluded on both sides. There is exactly one Dragon Egg per world, so the handover is the whole ceremony.
- **Into the Void** — Pure criteria, no detector. player_hurt_entity with entity_type player is native PvP; the distance min 1 guard is mandatory here because this trigger has no self-exclusion and an arrow that comes back down on its own shooter would otherwise satisfy it. The location predicate on the hurt player - dimension the_end plus position.y at most 20 - is the Enhanced Discoveries challenges/what_are_the_chances shape (entity.location.position.y range) and is what makes it 'into the void': End terrain sits well above Y=20, so a player there is already falling, and void damage does not start until Y=-64, leaving roughly two and a half seconds to land the shot. The damage predicate nests tags and direct_entity under type exactly as BACAP adventure/picador does. The Punch enchantment itself is not checked - no damage predicate carries the bow - so the description states the intent rather than a verified condition. Reworded from 'knock a player into the void': out_of_world deaths carry no killer, so nothing can attribute the kill.
- **Pearl Swap** — DETECTOR 2 of 5, function-granted (impossible criterion). The minecraft.used:minecraft.ender_pearl mirror is consumed UNGATED in bucket_fast — three commands for @a every tick, never behind the advancement gate — so it can never go stale and read a lifetime throw count as a fresh throw; seed_lines and start_extra both prime bacaptmh_pearlp. A throw opens a 100-tick window (bacaptmh_pw) and records the launch block in bacaptmh_px/py/pz via `data get entity @s Pos[n] 1`. While a window is live, detect/pearl_pair copies A's launch point and current position into shared #-scores, requires A to have moved more than 7 blocks in x or z (otherwise two players throwing pearls at their own feet side by side would pass every test below), and walks every OTHER player with a live window into detect/pearl_check, which grants both when B is within 3 blocks of A's launch point AND A is within 3 blocks of B's. Honest limitations: coordinates are integer-truncated, so tolerance near x=0 or z=0 is off by one block; and the check is on positions, not on the teleports themselves, so two players who threw pearls and then simply walked into each other's launch spots inside five seconds would also qualify.
- **Rocket Man** — minecraft:thrown_item_picked_up_by_entity on a Firework Rocket where both the thrower and the picker carry flags.is_flying with an Elytra in the chest slot - the elytra-gliding test BACAP uses in technical/divers_dozen_count and Enhanced uses in nether/flap_dont_fall, not creative flight. The one-block floor on the picker keeps a rocket you drop and fly back into from counting. Note the title sits very close to BACAP's own end/rocketman, which is also this advancement's parent.
- **Tag Team** — Every 0.5 s while an Ender Dragon exists in the End the detector samples the dragon's Health NBT; when it drops, every non-spectator, non-creative player within 128 blocks whose damage_dealt delta for that same window is positive gets a wounder stamp (bacaptmh_dgt = gametime). The delta comes from detect/sample, which runs UNGATED for every non-spectator every window and seeds its baseline the first time it sees a player, so a player who has never been sampled can never have their lifetime damage_dealt read as a fresh hit. When the dragon's Health reaches 0 (it lingers through the ~200-tick death animation, so there are many sampling chances) the advancement is granted to every player within 256 blocks whose stamp is under 2400 ticks old, provided there are at least two of them. Attribution is by damage-statistic delta plus proximity rather than a real damage-source hook, so hitting some other mob in the same half second the dragon is wounded could in principle tag a bystander; the two-minute stamp window means a player who stopped fighting long before the kill stops counting.
- **Tail Gunner** — SUBSTITUTION for Dragon Bait, which cannot be built from criteria. Dragon Bait needs the dragon's current target, and the Ender Dragon does not expose one: its aggro lives inside the phase instances (DragonStrafePlayerPhase and friends), not in Mob.getTarget, so the targeted_entity field this pack relies on in monsters/human_shield and monsters/avenged reads null on a dragon - the same reason the pack's own monsters/bait_and_switch note gives for going to a function. Counting End Crystal removals needs a counter too. This keeps the End challenge slot with a role split that criteria CAN prove: player_killed_entity on minecraft:ender_dragon (the trigger vanilla itself uses for minecraft:end/kill_dragon, so credit for the final blow is certain) plus the passenger-seat predicate - the ghast's first passenger, the pilot, must be another player at least 1 block off, so flying it yourself resolves to distance 0 and fails. Happy Ghasts in the End are already assumed by this pack in end/the_ferryman_cometh, so getting one there is a known-solved problem for the players. Honest caveats: it is the same 'act from the back seat of a Happy Ghast' predicate as Door Gunner and Cherry Picker, differing in dimension, target and act; and I cannot confirm from files here how well a Happy Ghast survives the dragon's knockback, which is part of why this is challenge-tier rather than goal.
- **The Ferryman Cometh** — Detector bacaptmh:detect/ferryman, dispatched from bucket_slow as/at every non-spectator who still lacks it, gated on 2 or more players online and on 'if dimension minecraft:the_end'. The function walks 'on vehicle ... on passengers' exactly as detect/carpool does (players are never written into a vehicle's Passengers NBT) and requires the vehicle to be a happy_ghast carrying at least two players; dismounting or losing the passenger sets the flag back to 0 and returns. Progress is then a three-stage flag driven by nested box selectors on @s, which are world-limited to the execution dimension: stage 1 inside a 192-block box over the centre, stage 2 in the open band between 600 and 900 blocks out, and the grant when they pass 1000 blocks out. An End Gateway drops a player from the centre straight to roughly a thousand blocks, so it can never touch the 600-900 band — that band is what makes the Gateway route unrewardable. The grant fires 'on vehicle on passengers' so every player aboard is credited, not just the one the loop happened to be running as.
- **Up and Away** — No detector. Each player's own effects_changed criterion latches the moment Levitation lands; the medium bucket turns that latch into a 20-medium-tick window - exactly the ten seconds a Shulker bullet grants - and revokes the latch again on the same pass, so the window is a live event and never a permanent flag. effects_changed re-fires on re-application, so a second bullet refreshes the window instead of letting it lapse mid-fight. The bucket then counts non-spectators holding a live window with `execute store result ... run execute if entity` (never a limit= selector) and grants to all of them at three. Levitation has no potion in vanilla, so in survival the only source is a Shulker bullet; that is why the source is not separately verified, and it is stated here rather than hidden. Counting is global rather than within a radius because a proximity test would need an `at @s` anchor and therefore a function; in practice three players levitating in the same ten seconds are in the same End city.
- **Wingman** — Pure criteria, AND-ed by default. 'gift' is an elytra picked up from another player's drop, with the thrower at distance >= 1 so self-drops are out. 'took_off' copies BACAP's own gliding test verbatim from end/divers_dozen — an elytra in the chest slot plus flags.is_flying — hung on minecraft:location, the periodic player trigger BACAP uses in potion/marine_marauder, so it resolves on the first 20-tick sample after take-off. The 'within a minute' clause is dropped: criteria cannot relate two events in time and the detector budget went elsewhere. Earned by the receiver, which is why the description is written from their side.

<details><summary>How these are detected</summary>

- **Act of God** — One criterion, the same trigger BACAP nether/electrifying_alliance uses to smite a ghast, with the victim predicate narrowed to a player at distance min 1 so you cannot count yourself. Channeling only works in a thunderstorm, so the weather clause needs no separate check. The description was changed from 'kill' to 'call lightning down onto': vanilla's lightning damage source carries no attacker - only the CHANNELED_LIGHTNING trigger receives the trident's owner - so player_killed_entity would never fire for a lightning kill and killing_blow.direct_entity would not match a lightning_bolt either. Asking for the kill would have made this unobtainable.
- **Aerial Superiority** — One criterion, no fallback and no requirement group: both the killer and the victim must have is_fall_flying set, the flag BACAP itself tests in weaponry/arrow_of_ultimate_obliteration. The trigger fires from LivingEntity.die before anything clears the victim's gliding flag, so the flag is still set when it is evaluated. The earlier equipment fallback was deleted outright: it accepted a victim who was merely off the ground, which any jump satisfies, and it made this criterion dead code.

The knighting blow is damage.taken between 0.01 and 0.99 - 'taken' rather than 'dealt' on purpose, because 'under half a heart' means the health the victim actually loses after armour, which is what makes this achievable at all: a Golden Sword's raw 4 damage can never be pushed under 1, but a well-armoured target absorbing it down to a fraction of a heart can. 'taken' is attested in weaponry/bow_spammer. The victim must be a player, sneaking (flags.is_sneaking, the shape used in technical/air_battle_detect), and at least 1 block away, because player_hurt_entity has no self-exclusion. The knight's own regalia is an entity_properties equipment predicate on mainhand and head, matching the equipment shape in enchanting/silent_but_deadly.

'armed' fires on the giver when another player picks up their thrown sword (recipient at distance >= 1, so retrieving your own drop does not count). 'slain' is minecraft:entity_killed_player — BACAP's nether/et_tu_brute shape — with the killer required to be a player holding a sword in mainhand; equipment on the killed-by entity predicate is BACAP's own usage from monsters/custom_boss_fight. Deliberately not claimed: that the sword that killed you is the sword you gave away. Stamping donor data into custom_data on the item, as the mechanism note suggested, needs a detector, and the pair of criteria already makes this impossible without a second player twice over.
- **Bare Knuckle Boxing** — Criterion plus detector. The 'kill' criterion is native PvP with a minecraft:is_player_attack killing_blow tag (the lancelot shape), so only a melee blow arms it; the impossible guard sits in its own requirements group, an AND with 'kill', so the criterion alone can never complete the advancement. bucket_fast then picks up any player showing kill=true,impossible=false - the same latch idiom redstone/trapped uses - and detect/bare_knuckle consumes the latch with 'advancement revoke ... only ... kill' FIRST, so a failed check cannot leave it set and re-fire every tick, then grants only if 'if items' finds nothing in container.*, any of the four armour slots or weapon.offhand. Because the fast bucket runs the tick after the kill and death drops carry a pickup delay, a killer who was empty-handed at the moment of the kill is still empty-handed on the check. Scope honestly narrowed from the original idea: only the KILLER's emptiness is verified. The victim is dead by the time anything can look at them, no entity predicate can test an empty inventory, and post-mortem their inventory is meaningless anyway, so 'you are both carrying nothing' became 'you are carrying nothing'.

Line one grants to a fresh killer whose freshly-dead neighbour within 64 blocks still carries a bounty; line two then stamps a 120-window (60-second) bounty on any fresh killer standing over a fresh corpse; line three ages the bounty. Documented weakening from the spare: the killer's identity is not carried anywhere a command can compare, so 'the player who killed YOUR teammate' is approximated by pairing a kill flag with a death flag inside 64 blocks, and the minimum is 2 players rather than 3.

The AND with 'you have damaged another player' is what makes it strictly multiplayer, since a lone player could otherwise blow themselves up with a charged creeper and pick up their own head. - **Ransom Note** — Six criteria, all six in their own AND groups. 'took_a_hostage' is player_killed_entity with entity_type minecraft:player, which is native on this server because PvP is on and needs no detector; ServerPlayer.awardKillScore skips the trigger when the killed entity is the killer, so it cannot be self-satisfied. The five give-backs are thrown_item_picked_up_by_entity - BACAP mining/diamonds_to_you, 'throw diamonds at another player' - each pinned to one Diamond piece with entity_type player and the distance min 1 guard on the receiver, which self-excludes because picking your own drop back up measures you against yourself at distance 0. The spare's real request, tracking the victim's dropped item entities by UUID and reconciling them inside 1200 ticks, was dropped: death drops are created by Inventory.dropAll with includeThrowerName false, so they carry no thrower at all and the pickup trigger never fires for them - that is why the returned gear has to be handed over deliberately rather than scavenged. Consequences of that: the criteria do not verify the five pieces went back to the same player you killed, nor that they are the gear they lost, and nothing is timed. The description says 'a full set', not 'their set'.
- **Riding Shotgun** — The batch's own spare, kept whole. Copied from this pack's weaponry/spear_joust, which copies BACAP monsters/lancelot and weaponry/hit_and_run: the blazeandcave:spear damage type tag IS the Spear charge attack (BACAP uses it for exactly that in hit_and_run and Impiled), the victim is anything in #blazeandcave:hostile_monsters, and the attacker must hold a #blazeandcave:spears item in main or off hand - the two variants sit in one OR group. Multiplayer proof: your vehicle is a minecraft:camel whose first passenger is a player. A Camel's two humps are roughly 1.2 blocks apart, but I could not verify that number from anything in this repo, so the guard is 0.3 rather than the usual 1 - self always measures exactly 0, so 0.3 still excludes the driver reading himself while leaving margin if the hump offsets are tighter than I think; it is the same reasoning the pack already applied to boat seats in biomes/crows_nest and weaponry/butch_and_sundance. NOT ENFORCED, from the spare: 'camel velocity non-zero'. BACAP gates hit_and_run with player.movement.speed min 8.0, but that floor is tuned to a downhill Minecart and I have no measurement for a dashing Camel's speed as seen by a passenger, so pinning a number risked making it unobtainable; the charge-attack damage type already implies the lunge. Unconfirmed: whether the back-hump passenger's own velocity is what the charge attack samples. If the game samples the rider rather than the mount, the driver would find this easier than the passenger - but the passenger predicate still forces two players either way.
- **Rope-a-Dope** — DETECTOR 4 of 5. `blocked` is entity_hurt_player with damage.blocked true — the shape BACAP uses in end/shouldnt_my_shield_levitate_too — and source_entity pinned to entity_type player with the distance absolute min 1 guard, which matters here because entity_hurt_player has no self-exclusion and an arrow you fired that comes back down on your own shield would otherwise count. The impossible criterion sits in its own requirements group. bucket_fast runs detect/rope_a_dope for anyone holding {blocked=true,impossible=false}: it revokes the advancement (consuming the flag, so it can only be re-set by the next real block), adds one to bacaptmh_rope, and grants at 20. The streak is reset in bucket_medium for any player whose bacaptmh_dmgd damage-dealt delta is positive — that delta is already computed ungated for everyone by the existing detect/sample earlier in the same bucket. Honest limitations: the counter does not track WHICH opponent, so twenty blocks split across two attackers still count, and 'without hitting back' is enforced as 'without dealing any damage to anything', mobs included, which is stricter than the original wording.
- **Second Wind** — Pure criteria, no detector. 'burn' is minecraft:used_totem (Enhanced Discoveries technical/used_totem). 'finish' is player_killed_entity on a player - native PvP, and no distance guard is used or wanted here because you cannot be your own killed entity and a melee kill often happens under one block. The 'still under the totem' half is carried by the totem's own effect signature: Regeneration II (amplifier at least 1) and Fire Resistance together, which a totem grants for 45 s and 40 s, plus an entity_scores read of bacaptmh_hp, a vanilla health objective, at 10 or below - five hearts. requirements are [[burn],[finish]], an AND of groups. Honest limitation: the two criteria are independent, so the totem need not have been burned on a blow from the player you then kill, nor in the same fight; a brewed Regeneration II plus a Fire Resistance potion can also produce the effect pair.
- **Secret Handshake** — Two criteria ANDed: you hurt a player (player_hurt_entity, entity_type player, distance min 1) and a player hurt you (entity_hurt_player with a source_entity predicate, the shape BACAP's Falling Out uses), both capped at 1.0 damage dealt, which is a bare fist. 'gave' is unsatisfiable alone, so the pair is impossible alone even though entity_hurt_player has no self-exclusion of its own; the distance min 1 on source_entity is there anyway so that your own arrow coming back down cannot arm 'took'. Substituted mechanism: the original wanted the two punches on the exact same tick, which needs a fast-bucket pairing function. Same-tick was dropped to stay inside the detector budget - this is a trade of punches, not a simultaneous one. The 1.0 cap can in principle also be met by a heavily cooldown-nerfed weapon swing or a hit on very high armour.
- **Shield Wall** — The damage_blocked_by_shield statistic only moves when a raised shield actually stops damage, so it doubles as 'blocking while being hit'. bacaptmh:detect/f2_events turns that mirror into the one-second bacaptmh_eblock flag for every player, gated on nothing, so the mirror can never go stale and holders of the advancement keep being flagged and keep counting towards other players' walls. detect/shield_wall then counts, for each flagged player, the flagged players within 8 blocks of him (himself included) and grants everyone who reaches three. The bucket entry counts players properly - scoreboard players set #f2players 0 then one add per non-spectator - instead of the @a[limit=3] form, which only ever tests for one player.
- **Snowball War** — Snowballs deal zero damage to players and Player.hurt returns early on zero damage, so no damage trigger ever fires; the projectile is watched instead. The watch starts from a player - bacaptmh:detect/f2_near runs as each non-spectator and looks for snowballs within 48 blocks of him - because a bare @e in a tick function only sees the dimension of the server command source, i.e. the overworld, and because an @e with no distance bound walks every loaded entity. Each snowball is then taken 'at @s on origin if entity @s[type=minecraft:player]', so the chain runs as the thrower and snow golem, dispenser and Breeze projectiles are skipped entirely; reaching the same snowball from two players in one tick is harmless because every step below is idempotent. The thrower carries a temporary owner tag while victims are picked, so he can never be one of his own three. Distinctness is now kept on the thrower, not on the victim: every player is given a permanent id at first sight and the thrower remembers the ids of the three players he has already hit in this window, so a second thrower can no longer overwrite the first thrower's record and let one player be counted twice. Every player inside the radius is offered rather than only the nearest, so an already-counted player standing in front no longer hides an uncounted one. The ten-second window is opened once, on the first hit, and is not pushed forward by later hits. No score is ever written to a snowball entity, so nothing accumulates in scoreboard.dat. Because the tick function runs before entities move, the last sample of a snowball is up to one tick of travel short of its impact, so the 1.6 block radius is what makes a real hit register; a snowball passing that close without landing also counts.
- **Spear Joust** — Pure criteria in BACAP's own monsters/lancelot and weaponry/hit_and_run shape, with no statistic mirror. The blazeandcave:spear damage type tag is the Spear charge attack, the victim must be a player whose vehicle is in #blazeandcave:all_horses, and the attacker must be holding a Spear from #blazeandcave:spears while riding a horse himself - so the blow is proved to have landed on a specific mounted player instead of being inferred from two riders damaging anything nearby. Mainhand and offhand are two criteria in one OR group, exactly as BACAP writes it. player_hurt_entity has no self-exclusion, but a Spear is a melee weapon - blazeandcave:spear resolves to the minecraft:spear damage type and there is no thrown spear entity - so unlike a trident it cannot come back down on its own user, and no distance guard is needed to keep this off a solo player.
- **Trident Tag** — The two criteria are hooks, not the completion condition: the third criterion is minecraft:impossible and the requirements are three separate AND groups, so the advancement can never complete on its own. bacaptmh:detect/trident_tag reads the hooks every 10 ticks and clears each one again with 'advancement revoke ... <criterion>' so it can fire afresh, which is what turns a permanent criterion into a dated event; the hit pass runs before the catch pass, so a hit and a catch landing in the same cycle still pair up. hit_player requires a projectile whose direct entity is a trident entity striking a player, which excludes a melee trident stab (its direct entity is the player), and it now carries entity.distance.absolute.min = 1, the self-guard BACAP uses in mining/diamonds_to_you: player_hurt_entity has no self-exclusion, so without it a trident thrown straight up and caught on the head satisfied the hook at distance 0 and one player could finish this alone. Any real hit on another player is at least a block away. It opens a four-second window; if the caught hook lands inside that window the advancement is granted and the window is cleared. Both hooks and the grant belong to the same player, so the revoke is only ever applied to players who do not hold the advancement and nobody can be locked out. The remaining looseness is on the catch side and is left deliberately: caught is 'an inventory change while a Loyalty trident is in the inventory', which a returning Loyalty trident always produces but which an unrelated item move within four seconds of a genuine hit also produces. Every tighter form was worse - a returning trident goes through Inventory.add and lands in the first free slot, not necessarily a hand, so an equipment predicate would make the advancement miss real catches, and a count-before/count-after check cannot be snapshotted before the return when both hooks are read in the same cycle. It over-grants a player who really did spear another player and is carrying a Loyalty trident; it is not solo-completable.
- **Wind Duel** — A wind burst deals no damage, so the projectile is watched. detect/f2_near runs as each non-spectator and looks for minecraft:wind_charge entities within 48 blocks of him - starting from a player instead of a bare @e is what makes the watch work outside the overworld and keeps the scan bounded - then takes each charge 'at @s on origin if entity @s[type=minecraft:player]', so a Breeze projectile (minecraft:breeze_wind_charge, a different entity type) and a dispenser-fired charge (no origin at all) are never followed. Credit is paired, not broadcast: every player carries a permanent id, the launched player records the id of the thrower who marked him, and the payout grants only the player whose id matches that record. The thrower carries an owner tag while victims are picked, so a self-launch is impossible, and creative, spectator and Elytra-gliding players are never marked. The rise check runs every tick, so the top of the arc cannot be missed. Two things make 'rose six blocks' mean 'was launched': the window is one second, not three - long enough for the burst's arc, too short to climb six blocks by jumping up a block stack, by ladder or by rocket - and the gliding test is re-applied every tick inside the window instead of only at marking time, so deploying an Elytra after being marked cancels the window rather than farming it. Heights are read as hundredths of a block (Pos[1] scaled by 100, threshold 600) because a plain read truncates towards zero and would misreport the rise by up to a whole block below Y=0. Six blocks is what a wind charge under a player's feet actually delivers; the original twenty blocks was more than the burst can produce, so that version would silently never have fired.
